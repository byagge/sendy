Refund & Billing Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

This Refund & Billing Policy explains how payments, subscriptions, trials, fees and refunds work for Sendy.

Sendy is operated by SENTO TRADE s.r.o., a company registered in the Czech Republic, IČO: 24279706.

1. Subscription-Based Digital Service

Sendy is a digital SaaS product provided on a subscription basis.

Available plans may include:

Starter; Pro; Private; other plans introduced from time to time.

Each plan may include a defined access period, region, source type, delivery option or feature set.

2. Trial

Sendy may offer a free trial period.

The trial is intended to allow users to test Sendy before purchasing a paid subscription.

The availability, length and limits of the trial may change from time to time. Current trial conditions are displayed on the Sendy website or inside the Sendy bot.

A trial may be limited, restricted or cancelled in case of abuse, fraud, prohibited use or technical risk.

3. Paid Plans

Paid plans are displayed on the website and/or inside the Sendy bot.

Current standard plans may include:

Starter — 14 days; Pro — 30 days; Private — 30 days.

Pricing may be updated from time to time. Any new price applies to future purchases and does not automatically change already activated subscription periods unless expressly stated or required by law.

4. Payment Providers

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. Payments may be processed through NOWPayments and other third-party payment providers. Provider and network fees may apply.

Depending on availability, Sendy may support:

  • crypto payments;
  • fiat payments;
  • card payments through third-party providers;
  • other payment methods.

Sendy does not store payment card details.

5. Crypto Payments

If you choose to pay with cryptocurrency, you are responsible for sending the correct amount, using the correct network and completing the payment before the payment window expires.

Crypto payments may involve:

  • network fees;
  • provider fees;
  • exchange rate changes;
  • confirmation delays;
  • blockchain transaction risks.

These fees and risks are not controlled by Sendy.

6. Service Fees and Payment Provider Fees

Payment provider fees, network fees or processing fees may be added to the final payable amount.

The final amount payable will be displayed before payment confirmation where technically possible.

7. Subscription Activation

Subscription access is activated after the payment provider confirms payment.

In some cases, activation may be delayed due to:

  • blockchain confirmation time;
  • payment provider delay;
  • incorrect payment amount;
  • expired invoice;
  • technical issue;
  • fraud or compliance review.

If payment was made but access was not activated, contact billing@sendy.direct with your payment ID or invoice details.

8. Refunds

Because Sendy provides immediate access to a digital subscription service, payments are generally non-refundable once the subscription has been activated and used.

Refunds may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis in situations such as:

  • duplicate payment;
  • confirmed payment processing error;
  • technical failure preventing access to the purchased service;
  • incorrect charge caused by our system;
  • other cases required by applicable law.

Refunds are not guaranteed for:

  • change of mind;
  • failure to use the service;
  • low number of matches caused by user-selected keywords or region;
  • incorrect user configuration;
  • lack of access to a Telegram chat;
  • Telegram restrictions or outages;
  • crypto network fees;
  • provider fees;
  • exchange rate differences;
  • expired invoice caused by delayed payment;
  • violation of Terms of Use.

9. Technical Issues

If you experience a technical issue, you must contact support as soon as possible.

We may attempt to resolve the issue by:

  • checking account status;
  • reactivating access;
  • correcting subscription status;
  • providing technical support;
  • extending access in appropriate cases;
  • reviewing refund eligibility.

10. Failed, Partial or Incorrect Payments

If a payment is failed, partial, expired or sent on the wrong network, access may not be activated automatically. We will review such cases where technically possible, but recovery may depend on the payment provider, blockchain network or payment method used.

11. Chargebacks and Disputes

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us first, we may suspend your account until the dispute is resolved.

For billing issues, contact:

billing@sendy.direct

12. Cancellation

You may stop using Sendy at any time.

Unless otherwise stated, cancellation stops future use but does not automatically create a refund for already activated subscription periods.

13. Contact

For billing questions: billing@sendy.direct

For support: support@sendy.direct
