2. What Sendy Does

Sendy helps users monitor selected Telegram chats and groups by words, phrases, regions and filters configured by the user.

The service is designed for legitimate business use, including:

  • lead discovery;
  • market monitoring;
  • brand monitoring;
  • competitor monitoring;
  • recruitment signals;
  • B2B opportunity detection;
  • private source monitoring.

When Sendy detects a message that matches the user’s configuration, it may send a notification to the user through Telegram.

3. User-Controlled Monitoring

Sendy works based on user configuration.

The user decides:

  • which words or phrases to monitor;
  • which region to select;
  • which chats or sources to connect;
  • whether to use public or private source modes, where available;
  • how to receive notifications.

Sendy does not independently decide which Telegram chats, words, phrases or regions should be monitored.

4. No Hidden Scraping

Sendy is designed as a Telegram-based monitoring tool that works through available Telegram functionality and user configuration.

Sendy does not perform hidden collection of private Telegram content outside the user’s configuration.

For connected chats, Sendy accesses content only where the relevant access is technically enabled through the product and where the user has configured the monitoring or added Sendy to a chat where they have the necessary access rights.

5. Ephemeral Processing

Sendy is designed around temporary technical processing.

Telegram message content is processed for the purpose of detecting matches and delivering notifications.

Sendy does not permanently archive full Telegram conversations or full message histories.

The service is designed to minimise retained data. Where data is retained, it is limited to what is necessary for the operation, security and billing of the service, such as configuration data, subscription data, match metadata, technical identifiers and message hashes/fingerprints.

6. GDPR Position

For customer account, billing, support and business administration data, SENTO TRADE may act as an independent data controller.

For Telegram group content processed according to user configuration, SENTO TRADE generally acts as a processor, while the user who configured the monitoring acts as the controller.

The user is responsible for determining the legal basis for the monitoring activity and for fulfilling any information obligations towards relevant data subjects where required by law.

Sendy provides technical infrastructure and processes data according to the user’s configuration and instructions.

8. Security Measures

Sendy is designed with privacy and security principles in mind.

Security measures may include:

  • encrypted transmission;
  • restricted access to production systems;
  • limited access by authorised personnel;
  • short-term technical processing;
  • internal confidentiality obligations;
  • data minimisation;
  • deletion procedures;
  • technical monitoring for abuse and security incidents.

9. DSA and Content Reporting

Sendy may technically process and temporarily handle content based on user instructions. To support compliance with the Digital Services Act and other applicable rules, Sendy provides a reporting channel for illegal content, misuse or source-related complaints.

If you believe that a connected source or use of Sendy involves illegal content or violates our rules, contact:

legal@sendy.direct

Please include:

  • your name and contact email;
  • description of the issue;
  • Telegram chat or message link, if available;
  • reason why the content or use is unlawful or violates the rules;
  • supporting information.

We may review the report and take appropriate action, such as restricting a source, removing a source from Sendy, suspending a user configuration, contacting the relevant user or responding to competent authorities.

10. Moderation

Sendy may apply moderation to protect the service and its users.

Moderation may apply to:

  • connected chats;
  • keywords and phrases;
  • message categories;
  • source quality;
  • spam or abuse;
  • prohibited content;
  • technical risk.

A chat or source may be rejected, restricted, suspended or removed if it creates legal, security, quality or abuse risks.

11. Payments and Payment Data

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers.

Sendy does not store payment card details.

Payment providers may process payment data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

For billing-related questions, contact:

billing@sendy.direct

12. Data Portability and Switching

Where required by applicable law, business users may request export of their portable account data.

Exportable data may include, where technically available:

  • account configuration;
  • user-defined words and phrases;
  • selected regions;
  • connected chat identifiers;
  • subscription metadata;
  • settings;
  • support records.

Sendy does not permanently store full Telegram message content and therefore cannot export full message archives that are not stored by the service.

Requests may be sent to:

legal@sendy.direct
