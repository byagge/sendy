Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SENTO TRADE s.r.o., operator of Sendy, collects, uses and protects personal data when you use the Sendy website, Telegram bot and related services.

Sendy is operated by:

Support: support@sendy.direct Legal and privacy contact: legal@sendy.direct

2. What Sendy Does

Sendy is a Telegram-based SaaS product that allows users to monitor selected Telegram chats and groups by user-defined words, phrases, regions and filters.

When a message matches the user’s configuration, Sendy may send a notification to the user through Telegram.

Sendy does not create, edit or modify Telegram messages. Sendy detects matches and delivers notifications based on user settings.

3. Our GDPR Roles

Sendy may process personal data in different roles depending on the context.

For customer account, billing, support, website and business administration data, SENTO TRADE generally acts as an independent data controller.

For Telegram group content processed according to a user’s monitoring configuration, SENTO TRADE generally acts as a data processor, and the user who configured the monitoring acts as the data controller.

The user decides which chats are connected, which words or phrases are monitored, which region is selected and how long the monitoring is used.

4. Data We Collect From Users

We may collect and process the following data about Sendy users:

Telegram user ID; Telegram username, if available; Telegram display name, if available; selected language; selected region; subscription status; selected plan; order and payment metadata; support messages; technical logs; user-defined words and phrases; connected chat identifiers; delivery settings; moderation status of connected sources; account configuration data.

5. Telegram Message Processing

Sendy may technically process Telegram message content from chats or groups where monitoring has been configured by a user.

Access is limited to chats and functionality enabled through the product and the user’s configuration. Message content is processed temporarily for match detection purposes only. Owners or administrators of Telegram communities may contact legal@sendy.direct to submit questions or removal requests.

This processing is performed only to:

  • detect whether a message matches user-defined words or phrases;
  • apply region and filter settings;
  • generate a notification;
  • deliver the notification to the user;
  • prevent duplicate notifications;
  • protect the service from abuse or prohibited content.

Sendy does not permanently store or archive the full content of Telegram messages.

Message content is processed temporarily and technically for match detection and notification delivery. The service is designed around ephemeral processing, data minimisation, limited access and short-term cache processing.

Where technically required, Sendy may store only limited metadata, such as:

  • matched keyword or phrase;
  • time of match;
  • selected region;
  • chat identifier;
  • message hash or fingerprint;
  • delivery status;
  • moderation status;
  • technical logs necessary for security and operation.

These records are not intended to reconstruct full Telegram conversations.

6. Data We Do Not Intend to Store

Sendy is not designed to permanently store:

  • full Telegram message archives;
  • full chat histories;
  • complete Telegram conversations;
  • payment card details;
  • unnecessary personal data of chat members;
  • profiles or behavioural scoring of Telegram users.

7. User Responsibility for Connected Chats

If you connect a Telegram chat or group to Sendy, you are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights, permissions and legal basis to do so.

If you are the user configuring monitoring, you are responsible for determining the legal basis for your monitoring activity and for providing information to relevant persons where required by law.

Sendy processes Telegram content only according to user configuration and does not independently decide which private chats should be monitored.

8. Payment Data

At this stage, subscription payments are accepted in USDT TRC20 via NOWPayments. Sendy does not store or process bank card details. Other payment methods may be introduced later.

Payment providers may process:

  • payment ID;
  • payment amount;
  • payment status;
  • crypto wallet or transaction metadata;
  • currency and network details;
  • invoice metadata;
  • fraud prevention and compliance data required by the provider.

Payment providers process payment data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Website Data and Cookies

When you visit our website, we may process limited technical data such as:

IP address; browser type; device type; operating system; pages visited; time of visit; referrer; cookie identifiers, if cookies are used.

We use this data to operate the website, secure the service, analyse basic performance and improve user experience.

If analytics or tracking tools are used, they will be described in the cookie banner or cookie policy where required.

10. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the context, we may process personal data based on:

  • performance of a contract;
  • legitimate interests;
  • compliance with legal obligations;
  • user consent, where required;
  • the user’s documented instructions, where Sendy acts as processor.

For Telegram content processed on behalf of a user, the user is responsible for determining the appropriate legal basis.

11. How We Use Data

We use data to:

  • provide the Sendy service;
  • create and manage subscriptions;
  • detect matches;
  • deliver notifications;
  • process payments;
  • provide support;
  • secure the service;
  • prevent abuse;
  • moderate prohibited content;
  • comply with legal obligations;
  • respond to valid legal requests;

12. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • payment providers;
  • hosting and infrastructure providers;
  • email and support service providers;
  • legal, tax or accounting advisors;
  • competent authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

We do not use Telegram message content for unrelated advertising, profiling or resale.

13. International Transfers

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we take appropriate measures required by applicable data protection law. Such measures may include contractual safeguards, technical measures and provider due diligence.

14. Security Measures

Sendy is designed with privacy and security principles in mind.

Security measures may include:

  • encrypted transmission;
  • restricted access to production systems;
  • limited operational access;
  • short-term technical processing;
  • internal confidentiality rules;
  • logging and monitoring for security;
  • access control;
  • data deletion procedures.

15. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account, subscription and billing metadata may be retained for legal, accounting, tax and operational reasons.

User-defined words, phrases and monitoring configurations may be deleted or deactivated after subscription expiry, account deletion or user request, unless retention is required by law.

Telegram message content is not permanently stored or archived by Sendy.

16. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • request correction;
  • request deletion;
  • object to processing;
  • restrict processing;
  • request data portability;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Requests may be sent to legal@sendy.direct.

17. Requests Relating to Telegram Group Content

If you are a member of a Telegram group and your data has been processed through Sendy based on another user’s monitoring configuration, Sendy may not be the controller of that processing.

In such cases, we may forward your request to the Sendy user who configured the monitoring, where appropriate and technically possible, because that user determines the purpose and means of the monitoring.

18. Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account data or monitoring configuration by contacting support@sendy.direct or legal@sendy.direct.

Some data may need to be retained for legal, tax, billing, fraud prevention or dispute resolution purposes.
